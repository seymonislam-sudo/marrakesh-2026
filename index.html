          Worth knowing
          • Strangers who say a street is closed and offer to show you the way will want paying. Say la, shukran and keep walking.
          • Haggle in the souks, not in restaurants. Open at a third to a half of the first price, and be happy to walk away.
          • Petit taxis are the beige ones. Agree the fare or insist on the meter before you get in. The riad can book the airport and desert runs.
          • Tip about 10% in restaurants, and leave something for Soumia and the riad team on Sunday.
          • Hotels, licensed restaurants and rooftop bars serve alcohol; street cafés don't. Don't drink in the street.
          • Some souk stalls shut over Friday prayers, roughly 12:30 to 14:30.
          • Ask before photographing people.
          • Sockets are European two-pin, 220 V.
          • UK passports don't need a visa for a short stay.
