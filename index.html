        Worth knowing
        • "That way's closed, follow me." It isn't. Friendly strangers offering directions will want paying; a firm la, shukran and keep walking. Offline Google Maps of the medina helps.
        • Haggling is expected in the souks, not in restaurants. Start around a third to a half of the first price and be ready to walk away. Smile throughout.
        • Taxis: petit taxis are beige. Insist on the meter or agree the fare before you get in. Easiest of all, get the riad to book airport and desert transfers.
        • Tipping: around 10% in restaurants; a few dirhams for small help; and a pooled tip for Soumia and the riad staff when we leave.
        • Drinks: alcohol is easy to find in hotels, licensed restaurants and rooftop bars, but not in street cafés. Don't drink in public.
        • Fridays: some souk stalls close over midday prayers, roughly 12:30–14:30.
        • Photos: ask before snapping people or stallholders. Very on-brand for this crew.
        • Plugs: European two-pin (type C/E), 220 V. Bring an adaptor.
        • Passports: UK passport holders don't need a visa for a short trip. Keep the passport on you or locked in the riad.
